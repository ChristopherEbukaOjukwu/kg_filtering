# H2 Robustness Results

This is a clean and decisive robustness result.

H2 does not just survive across thresholds. Its central finding strengthens monotonically as filters become more stringent.

---

## What the Robustness Table Shows

### Fold-Over-Independence

The simplest model-free version of the H2 finding is the fold-over-independence measure.

| Threshold Setting | Fold Over Independence |
|---|---:|
| Permissive: F1 ≥ 0.2 | 4.2× |
| Default: F1 ≥ 0.5 | 13.0× |
| Stringent: F1 ≥ 0.8 | 25.8× |
| Stringent F2: ≥ 3 sources | 56.3× |

The fully stacked filter cell contains between:

```text
4× and 56×
```

more pairs than independent filtering would predict, depending on the threshold setting.

This means:

```text
The compounding effect is present across every setting tested.
```

Therefore, the H2 claim is robust.

---

# F1 × F2 Interaction Coefficient

The F1 × F2 interaction coefficient is the most diagnostic H2 result.

| Threshold Setting | β | exp(β) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Permissive | 2.00 | 7.4× |
| Default | 3.30 | 27.1× |
| Stringent | 10.46 | 35,030× |
| Stringent F2 | 4.97 | 143× |

---

## Interpreting the Stringent F1 Result

The stringent F1 setting produces an extreme coefficient:

```text
β = 10.46
exp(β) = 35,030×
```

However, this value is driven by sparse cells, including three empty cells and the `+0.5` continuity correction.

In practical terms, this means:

```text
The interaction is so strong that it cannot be precisely estimated.
```

A safer interpretation is:

```text
Effectively, all stringent-F1 survivors are also F2 survivors.
```

Therefore, in the chapter, report this as:

```text
β ≥ 10
```

rather than treating `35,030×` as a precise value.

---

## Interpreting the Permissive Result

The permissive setting is the least extreme:

```text
β = 2.00
exp(β) = 7.4×
```

But this is still far above the preregistered H2 threshold:

```text
|β| ≥ 0.3
```

Even at the most permissive threshold, F1 and F2 co-survival is approximately:

```text
7× higher than the independence baseline
```

So H2 is robust even under lenient filtering.

---

# Note on F1 × F4 at the Permissive Threshold

One unexpected pattern is worth flagging.

At the permissive threshold:

```text
F1 ≥ 0.2
```

the F1 × F4 interaction is larger than the F1 × F2 interaction.

| Threshold Setting | Dominant Interaction |
|---|---|
| Permissive | F1 × F4 |
| Default | F1 × F2 |
| Stringent | F1 × F2 |

At the permissive setting:

```text
F1 × F4: β = 3.67
exp(β) = 39×
```

This is larger than F1 × F2 at the same threshold.

---

## Interpretation

When F1 is lenient, many non-protein-coding pairs can enter the survivor set.

For example:

```text
lncRNAs or other non-coding targets with modest association scores
```

This gives F4 more room to act.

At this threshold, F4 captures the strong filtering effect of:

```text
Is this target protein-coding, given that it already has moderate association evidence?
```

When F1 becomes stricter, most F1-passers are already protein-coding.

Therefore, F4 has less marginal work left to do, and F1 × F2 becomes the dominant interaction.

The key point is:

```text
The dominant interaction shifts with threshold, but strong non-linear compounding remains present across all settings.
```

# Chapter Write-Up Version

> To assess threshold dependence, we re-ran the factorial analysis at three F1 thresholds spanning permissive, default, and stringent association-score cutoffs, as well as one alternative F2 specification requiring at least three sources. The fully stacked F1 ∧ F2 ∧ F4 cell contained between 4.2× and 56× more pairs than independent filtering would predict, with over-representation increasing monotonically with filter stringency. All three pairwise interaction coefficients remained large and positive across all settings, with |β| ≥ 1.2 in every case. The dominant interaction shifted with threshold: F1 × F4 dominated under permissive filtering, while F1 × F2 dominated under default and stringent filtering. However, the overall pattern of non-linear compounding was invariant across the tested parameter range.

This result pre-empts the objection that the H2 finding depends on the specific choice of:

```text
F1 ≥ 0.5
```

# Summary

The robustness results strengthen the H2 claim.

The core finding is not threshold-dependent.

Across permissive, default, stringent, and stricter multi-source settings, stacked filters retain far more target–disease pairs than independence would predict.

In simple terms:

```text
The filters do not combine additively.
They compound.
```
