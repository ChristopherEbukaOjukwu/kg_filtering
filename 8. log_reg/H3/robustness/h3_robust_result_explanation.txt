# H3 PPI Robustness Check

This is a strong robustness result, with one finding that tightens the H3 story.

---

## What the Robustness Check Shows

Overall, the H3 result is robust to PPI source choice.

| Metric | PPI = All / STRING-Inclusive | PPI = Curated |
|---|---:|---:|
| AUC | 0.7714 | 0.7736 |
| Log-likelihood | -9,142.6 | -9,121.0 |
| Residual correlation | — | 0.997 |
| Top-100 under-survivor overlap | — | 83% |

The two models give nearly identical predictions.

Key robustness signals:

```text
Residual correlation = 0.997
AUC difference ≈ 0.002
Top-100 under-survivor overlap = 83%
```

This means the under-survivor population is highly stable across PPI source choices.

The visible differences in the top-20 lists are mostly reorderings of the same broad gene class rather than substitutions of one biological population for another.

# Unexpected Wrinkle: PPI Coefficient Sign Flip

One interesting result is the sign flip on the PPI coefficient.

| PPI Variable | β | p-value |
|---|---:|---:|
| `log_ppi_all` | +0.145 | 2.6 × 10⁻⁶ |
| `log_ppi_curated` | -0.140 | 6.2 × 10⁻¹⁶ |

In the STRING-inclusive model, higher PPI degree predicts higher survival.

In the curated-PPI model, higher PPI degree predicts lower survival.

This is not contradictory.

The two PPI networks measure different things.

---

## Interpretation of STRING-Inclusive PPI Degree

STRING degree reflects broad interactor richness, including:

```text
text-mined interactions
predicted interactions
known interactions
database-derived interactions
```

Because STRING includes text-mined and predicted edges, its degree is strongly correlated with research attention.

Well-studied genes tend to have more characterized partners, more inferred partners, and more text-mined associations.

Therefore, in the STRING-inclusive model:

```text
PPI degree partly behaves like another attention proxy.
```

This explains the positive coefficient.

---

## Interpretation of Curated PPI Degree

Curated PPI degree, restricted to sources such as:

```text
IntAct
SIGNOR
Reactome
```

reflects experimentally supported or curated molecular interactions.

This is closer to:

```text
structural hub-ness
```

rather than broad research visibility.

After controlling for publication attention using:

```text
log(n_publications + 1)
```

the remaining curated PPI signal appears to capture whether a gene is a biological interaction hub.

The negative coefficient suggests:

```text
structurally hub-like genes are less likely to survive the evidence-quality filters than their publication attention would predict.
```

# How This Sharpens H3

The curated-PPI result strengthens the H3 interpretation.

The key idea is:

```text
Highly connected hub proteins are less likely to survive evidence-quality filters than their attention levels predict.
```

A likely reason is that hub genes participate broadly across many biological processes and disease contexts.

Their evidence may be:

```text
broad
diffuse
pleiotropic
spread across many diseases
not concentrated into specific multi-source disease anchors
```

But the filters reward:

```text
focused
source-diverse
disease-specific
corroborated target–disease evidence
```

So H3 becomes more precise.

The result is not simply:

```text
filters retain well-studied genes
```

Instead:

```text
filters retain genes whose evidence is focused and disease-specific,
while structurally hub-like genes may under-survive because their evidence is diffuse.
```

> To assess whether the H3 finding depended on the choice of PPI source, we refit the structural baseline using curated interactions only from IntAct, SIGNOR, and Reactome, excluding STRING’s predicted and text-mined edges. # Summary

The H3 result is robust to PPI source choice.

The main under-survivor pattern does not depend on STRING.

Using curated PPI sharpens the interpretation:

```text
Publication attention predicts survival, but imperfectly.
Some highly studied, structurally hub-like genes under-survive because their evidence is broad and diffuse rather than focused and source-diverse.
```

The strongest conceptual takeaway is:

```text
Filtered gene–disease knowledge graphs may privilege evidence concentration over biological connectedness.
```
