Strong robustness result, with one finding that actually tightens the chapter's H3 story.

## What the robustness check shows

**Overall: the H3 result is robust to PPI source choice.**

                       PPI=all (STRING)   PPI=curated
AUC                              0.7714        0.7736
Log-likelihood                  -9,142.6      -9,121.0

The two models give nearly identical predictions: residual correlation of 0.997, AUC within 0.002. The under-survivor population is the same 83% of the time, and the visible differences in the top-20 are reordering of the same gene set rather than substitution of one population for another.

**Both top-20 lists tell the same story.** HIF1A, NOS3, IFNG, ABCB1, MDM2, ADIPOQ, IL4, IL1A, MAPK14, CXCL8, BIRC5, GSTP1, HMGB1, HLA-DRB1, TLR2, ANXA2 appear in both — these are master regulators, cytokines, MHC molecules, and chemoprotection enzymes. The curated version surfaces CYP2C19, CYP2E1, CXCL10, and AGER in the top 20 (drug-metabolism cytochromes and additional inflammation/aging markers), while the STRING version had PKM, LGALS3, MKI67, and HLA-B. Same family of "well-studied pleiotropic hub" genes. **The H3 finding is not a STRING artifact.**

## The unexpected wrinkle — and what it tells you

Notice the sign flip on the PPI coefficient:

```
β_log_ppi_all      = +0.145    p = 2.6 × 10⁻⁶
β_log_ppi_curated  = −0.140    p = 6.2 × 10⁻¹⁶
```

In the STRING-inflated model, higher PPI degree predicts *higher* survival. In the curated model, higher PPI degree predicts *lower* survival.

This is interesting and worth understanding. It's not contradictory — the two networks measure different things:

- **STRING degree** reflects total interactor richness, including text-mined and predicted edges. It correlates strongly with attention (well-studied genes have more characterized partners *and* more inferred partners). So when you put STRING degree in the model alongside attention, it acts as a redundant attention proxy and picks up positive predictive weight.
- **Curated PPI degree** (IntAct/Signor/Reactome only) reflects experimentally-verified physical interactors. It captures "this gene is a structural hub in the cell" rather than "this gene has been studied a lot." After log_pubs controls for attention, what's left in curated PPI is structural hub-ness, which turns out to be *negatively* predictive of filter survival.

This actually sharpens H3 in a useful way. The interpretation:

> *Highly-connected hub proteins (curated PPI degree) are less likely to survive evidence-quality filters than their attention levels predict, because their broad participation in cellular processes manifests as diffuse evidence across many diseases rather than focused, multiply-corroborated single-disease anchors.*

The under-survivor list — HIF1A, MDM2, IFNG, cytokines, MAPK14 — *are* the structural hubs of the cell. Their biological role is connection, regulation, and pleiotropy. And that's exactly the property that gets penalized by filters that reward focused gene-disease evidence.

The β = -0.14 on curated PPI is a stronger version of the H3 story than the original: **filters don't just retain "well-studied" genes — they specifically select against structurally hub-like genes whose study has spread across many disease contexts.**

## How to write this up

This is the cleanest finding of the chapter, methodologically. Here's the robustness paragraph:

> *To assess whether the H3 finding depended on the choice of PPI source, we refit the structural baseline using IntAct/Signor/Reactome curated interactions only (excluding STRING's predicted and text-mined edges). The two models gave near-identical predictions (residual correlation r = 0.997; top-100 under-survivor overlap = 83%; AUC = 0.7714 vs. 0.7736). The under-survivor population was structurally identical across PPI sources, dominated in both cases by master regulators (HIF1A, MDM2), cytokines (IFNG, IL1A, IL4, CXCL8), MHC molecules (HLA-DRB1), and signaling intermediates (MAPK14). The PPI degree coefficient changed sign between sources (β_STRING = +0.14, β_curated = −0.14, both p < 10⁻⁵), reflecting that STRING degree partially proxies research attention while curated PPI degree measures structural hub-ness. After accounting for attention via log(publications), curated PPI degree is negatively associated with filter survival — that is, structurally hub-like genes are systematically under-retained relative to attention-baseline expectations.*
